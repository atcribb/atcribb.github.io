title: "Life and Planet 2026 and ESSG updates"
date: 2026-07-20
---

We have just finished another successful Life and Planet meeting. Many thanks as always to the Geological Society of London and Burlington House for hosting nearly 100 researchers from across the globe who came together to discuss the co-evolution of life and the environment.

I am also excited to announce that, while I have stepped down from my role as Secretary, I have been elected to a three-year term as the new ESSG Vice Chair! I will also be continuing my role as EDI Chair until 2027. I am excited about the work that ESSG is doing to bring together the geobiology and Earth systems science communities from around the world, and I can't wait to continue to liaise with the Geological Society of London on work that's helping the community reach its goals.
