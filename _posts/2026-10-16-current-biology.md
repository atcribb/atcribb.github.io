From the E6 RCN team, we have published a new review on ecosystem engineering through the Ediacaran-Cambrian transition for the new Current Biology "Life and Earth" special issue. We outline key ecosystem engineering innovations that emerged during this time interval, rooted in the dramatic planetary-scale changes happening on Earth in the late Neoproterozoic and earliest Phanerozoic. Check out the paper <a href=https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2026.08.056>in Current Biology here</a>.
