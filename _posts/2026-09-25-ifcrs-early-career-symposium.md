title: "5th Annual IFCRS Early Career Symposium"
date: 2026-09-25

I am pleased to be organizing again this year the International Fossil and Coral Reef Society Early Career Symposium on 19 November. We are excited to highlight 10 early career researchers from across the world who are working on a range of topics, from the earliest microbialite reefs to conservation management strategies in our future. This year, we are also hosting meet-the-lab sessions with PIs and online coffee break chats about career advice and emerging research frontiers. Keep an eye on IFCRS.com for news and udpates about how to register and attend!
